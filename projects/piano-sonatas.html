Piano Sonatas

View all pieces in this project

Most of my projects have some unifying element, like my pieces than respond to ragtime, or hymn settings, or tuning experiments. I began the piano sonatas project in 2026 as a way to write pieces under an umbrella title that aren't intend to form a set or a unified project, to push back against my own familiar ways of composing—to try new methods (or old, as the case may be, in this case a return to pencil and paper, as was my habit in my teenage years) and to break my habits of form. The focus is on discontinuities, on pieces that attempt to be distinct from each other, that try not to belong with anything else. See my Minifesto before the piano sonatas blog post for further elucidation on these concepts.

Gearing up for the project, Tammy heard me talking about pencils. At my birthday, I unwrapped package after package consisting of pencils, various brands and types to try out.
