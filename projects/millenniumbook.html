Millennium Book

View all pieces in this project

This series of brief preludes for organ solo was intended to reach to 1,000 pieces (thus the title), grouped in volumes of 20 pieces each. I got as far as the first three volumes—60 pieces total—but I’m sticking with the title, which has religious overtones, as does much of the music in these volumes.

As with many of my projects, the Millennium Book was a reaction against some compositional habits I had developed. Rather than being long, difficult, complex solo works, I wanted to write pieces that are short, simple(r), and functional in a variety of contexts, like worship or pedagogical settings. The most significant constraint I imposed was no meter changes.
