New Ragtime

In 2023, I decided to take a break from “serious” music and turn back the clock to a childhood interest of mine: ragtime piano. The project has resulted in five volumes of ragtime-inspired pieces (and one volume of waltzes), taking inspiration not only from ragtime greats like Scott Joplin and Joseph Lamb, but also from musicians such as Jelly Roll Morton, Fats Waller, and Gene Harris. It's been loads of fun, although the pieces are more fit for the concert stage than for the ragtime festival or local cafe.

The resulting project consists of four volumes of Rags 'n Things (15 titles each), one volume of Ragatelles (9 pieces), and a volume of waltzes.
